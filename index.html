The Real Problem

You've been saying
"when I'm good enough"
for over a year.

And every time you almost start, something happens — a better designer appears on your timeline, your work doesn't come out exactly right, and the deadline moves again.

The honest truth is this: the gap between where you are and where you want to be isn't a skill gap. It's a decision gap. And the four lies below are what's keeping it open.

  • "I need to be better first."
    There is a skill level at which clients will pay you. You're likely already there.
  • "Who would pay me when real designers exist?"
    Clients don't hire the best designer. They hire the one who shows up.
  • "I don't have a degree so I'm not legitimate."
    The market doesn't ask for your transcript. It asks for your portfolio and your price.
  • "I'll start when I have better equipment."
    You need a profile, three pieces, and the willingness to send five messages. That's it.
